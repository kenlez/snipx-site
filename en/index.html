    Features

    Always there in the menu bar

    Capture, annotate, record, and scroll-capture — all from the menu bar in one tap.

    Lives in the menu bar

    Click to open, never in the way

    SnipX lives quietly in the corner of your menu bar. No main window to open — a single click reveals the capture / recording entry points.

    • Capture, recording, annotation, settings — one tap each
    • Never steals focus or breaks your flow
    • Native menu bar icon that follows macOS design

    Editable shortcuts

    Global shortcuts, freely assigned

    Capture, recording, annotation, scroll-capture — each action gets its own shortcut, with warnings before any conflict.

    • Single keys / combos / modifiers
    • Conflict detection, one-click reset
    • Independent shortcuts for record & capture, annotation on demand

    Four capture modes

    Region / window / full screen / scrolling

    Pixel-perfect output, automatically rendered at your display scale. Window capture auto-detects app window edges; scrolling capture stitches seamlessly.

    • Region: drag to select, pixel-accurate
    • Window: auto-detect, transparent background
    • Full screen: multi-display at once
    • Scrolling: auto-stitched, no seams

    On-device OCR (new in 1.0.0)

    One-tap text recognition from your captures

    Powered by the Apple Vision framework, SnipX recognizes Chinese, English, and mixed Chinese/English text directly on your Mac. Edit or copy the result — nothing is uploaded.

    • Simplified & Traditional Chinese, English, and mixed
    • Fully on-device, never leaves your Mac
    • Edit, copy, or cancel after recognition
    • Great for extracting text from captures

    Annotation tools

    Rectangle / arrow / text / mosaic

    Annotate the moment you capture. Move, delete, undo, redo anything. Crop, mosaic, and a color picker — all built in.

    • Rectangle, ellipse, arrow, pen, text
    • Mosaic sensitive details
    • Undo / redo / crop / color picker

    Local MP4 recording · SnipX Pro

    Countdown · system audio + mic · auto-recovery

    Built on ScreenCaptureKit for fully local recording. Capture system audio and mic simultaneously, with countdown, disk protection, and crash recovery. Trim head & tail and export — no third-party editor needed.

    • Region / window / display, three modes
    • System audio + mic, single mixed track
    • Countdown & live status, no interruption
    • Crash recovery preserves the last clip
    • Trim head & tail, export a new MP4

    Recording & GIF capture unlock with SnipX Pro (one-time purchase, price set in the App Store). Capture, annotation, and scrolling capture stay free, forever.

    Zero data leaving your Mac

    Fully offline

    Capture, recording, and OCR are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no telemetry. Purchases and restore go through Apple.

    • Purchases & restore may need internet
    • No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync
    • No third-party SDK, no trackers
    • Local-first storage, friendly to open source

    About

    About SnipX

    Built native

    Written in Swift + AppKit + SwiftUI + ScreenCaptureKit + AVFoundation + Carbon. No WebView, no Electron, no wrapper layer.

    Privacy first

    All processing stays on your Mac — no SnipX account required. Purchases go through Apple; sharing is always your choice. Details in Privacy Policy。

    SnipX Pro

    Capture, annotation, scrolling capture, and pinning stay free forever. A one-time purchase unlocks MP4 recording and GIF capture. Price set in the App Store.

    Continuously maintained

    Current version 1.0.0。Every release goes through manual testing, unit tests, and functional tests. Need help? support page。
